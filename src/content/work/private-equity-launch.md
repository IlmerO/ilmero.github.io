## Context

LIQID is a digital wealth manager in Berlin. When it launched Private Equity NXT, I owned the reporting behind it.

## What I did

I built the models from the raw layer up to the reports used by the C-level, from scratch. The reporting tracked about €120M in allocations.

Around the same time I took over the data stack itself, with ETL and orchestration in Python, Docker, Airflow and dbt on BigQuery, and became responsible for about ten models fed by external APIs: Google Search, Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn, HubSpot and QPlix.
