## Context

Hotmart is one of the largest creator economy platforms in Latin America. I was on the Core Product team, in the vertical focused on creators, while the company rolled out a new platform.

## What I did

- Mapped and modeled the adoption data for the new platform, opt-in, engagement, retention and profitability, together with the front-end and back-end engineers.
- Built the ETL behind those metrics.
- Built an RFM analysis to segment creators by how recently, how often and how much they sold.
- Worked on integrations with the platform's webhooks.
